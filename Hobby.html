MIMAROPA Integrated Network of DOST Scholars

MIMAROPA Integrated Network of DOST Scholars (MINDS) is a DOST-SEI Scholars' organization that aims to provide a platform for scholars and aspiring scholars to connect, collaborate, and engage in various activities that promote personal and professional growth. Our mission is to foster a sense of community among students in the MIMAROPA region, and to share knowledge, experiences, and opportunities for development.

This website includes DOST-SEI & DOST MIMAROPA news, and events. With added features of a Social and Event Calendar to display pending and upcoming events of the organization. Moreover, a dedicated community page that provides users access or links to communities of DOST MIMAROPA such as Discord and Facebook community.
